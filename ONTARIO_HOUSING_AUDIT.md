# ONTARIO HOUSING & TENANCY AUDIT (2026)

## 1. Tenancy Law & Financial Rules (*Residential Tenancies Act*, RTA)
- **Damage Deposits:** 100% ILLEGAL under RTA section 105. Landlords cannot request, demand, or accept damage deposits.
- **Permitted Deposits:** First month's rent (upon lease inception) and Last Month's Rent (LMR) deposit (held to cover final month's rent only).
- **Rent Control Cap:** Dwellings first occupied before November 15, 2018 are subject to the annual provincial guideline cap (2.5%). Dwellings first occupied after November 15, 2018 have **no rent control**.
- **Pet Clauses:** Void under RTA section 14.
