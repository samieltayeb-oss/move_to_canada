# BRITISH COLUMBIA RESEARCH LEDGER (2026 STATUTORY AUDIT)
## Primary Sources, Regulations & Verified Data Points

**Target Province:** British Columbia (BC)  
**Cities Examined:** Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond, Coquitlam, Victoria  
**Review Status:** Verified against official provincial & federal registries

## 1. Taxation & Statutory Deductions
- **Personal Income Tax (Budget 2026 Adjusted):**
  - 5.60% on taxable income up to $50,363
  - 7.70% on taxable income from $50,363.01 to $100,728
  - 10.50% on taxable income from $100,728.01 to $115,648
  - 12.29% on taxable income from $115,648.01 to $140,430
  - 14.70% on taxable income from $140,430.01 to $190,405
  - 16.80% on taxable income from $190,405.01 to $265,545
  - 20.50% on taxable income over $265,545
- **Basic Personal Amount:** $13,216 CAD (credit = $740.10)
- **BC Tax Reduction Credit:** Max $690, phasing out at 3.56% of net income over $25,570 (fully phased out at $44,952).
- **Dual Sales Tax Structure:** 5% Federal GST + 7% Provincial PST (separate taxes, not harmonized).
  - PST Exemptions: Basic groceries, children's clothing/footwear, school supplies, human-powered bicycles/e-bikes, prescription drugs.

## 2. Healthcare (MSP & Fair PharmaCare)
- **Waiting Period:** Mandatory statutory waiting period enforced.
  - Formula: **Balance of arrival month + 2 consecutive calendar months**.
  - Example: Arrival on July 15 $\rightarrow$ MSP coverage begins October 1.
  - **MANDATORY Bridge Insurance:** Private travel/newcomer emergency insurance must be purchased to cover the initial ~75-90 days.
- **Premiums:** $0. Eliminated since January 1, 2020 for Canadian citizens, PRs, and work permit holders.
- **Enrolment:** Apply via Health Insurance BC (HIBC), then attend an ICBC office for BC Services Card photo/identity verification.
- **Telehealth:** HealthLink BC (dial 811, 24/7 nurses, pharmacists from 5pm to 9am, dietitians, 130+ languages).
- **Fair PharmaCare:** Income-tested prescription drug assistance based on family net income from 2 tax years prior. Deductible is 2%–3% of net income over thresholds, then PharmaCare pays 70% until family maximum (3%–4% of income) is reached, after which 100% is covered.

## 3. Driving & Non-Reciprocal Exchange (ICBC)
- **Grace Period:** 90 days to drive on valid foreign licence.
- **Licence Surrender:** Mandatory physical surrender of foreign licence upon applying for a BC licence.
- **Foreign Experience Credit:**
  - Applicants with 2+ years of verified non-learner driving experience (Saudi Moroor abstract translated by ICBC-approved translator) bypass the Graduated Licensing Program (GLP Class 7) directly to take the **Class 5 road test**.

## 4. Housing & Rental Benchmarks
- **Tenancy Law:** Security deposit is legal (capped at 50% of one month's rent). Last month's rent deposit is not collected upfront. Universal annual rent increase cap (3.0% - 3.5%) applies to all rental dwellings.
- **3-Bedroom Rental Benchmarks:**
  - Vancouver: $3,900 – $4,800 (CMHC occupied: $2,820)
  - Burnaby: $3,400 – $4,000 (CMHC occupied: $2,600)
  - Surrey: $2,800 – $3,300 (CMHC occupied: $2,300)
  - Richmond: $3,200 – $3,800 (CMHC occupied: $2,500)
  - Coquitlam: $3,100 – $3,600 (CMHC occupied: $2,400)
  - Victoria: $2,800 – $3,300 (CMHC occupied: $2,886)

## 5. Transit Systems
- **TransLink (Metro Vancouver):**
  - Zones apply to SkyTrain and SeaBus during peak weekday hours: 1-Zone $2.85, 2-Zone $4.20, 3-Zone $5.40 (Compass).
  - All buses are 1-zone flat fare across the entire region at all times.
  - Off-peak travel (after 6:30 PM weekdays and all day weekends) is 1-zone across all SkyTrain lines.
  - Monthly passes: 1-Zone $117.20, 2-Zone $156.70, 3-Zone $211.65.
  - Kids 12 and under ride 100% free under the provincial "Get on Board" program.
- **BC Transit (Victoria):** $3.00 single fare ($85.00 monthly pass). Automatic daily fare capping via Umo app/card triggers at $6.00 (2 rides = unlimited rides for remainder of day). Kids 12 and under ride free.

## 6. School Boards & Islamic Education
- **Public School Districts:** Surrey SD36 (largest in BC), Vancouver SD39, Burnaby SD41, Richmond SD38, Coquitlam SD43, Greater Victoria SD61 ($0 tuition).
- **Islamic Schools (Independent Group 1 Funded):**
  - Surrey Muslim School (BCMA): ~$3,957/yr (1st child), sibling discounts down to ~$2,700/child
  - Iqra Islamic School (Surrey): ~$4,275/yr
  - Az-Zahraa Islamic Academy (Richmond): ~$3,800 – $4,400/yr
  - *Note:* Under the *BC Independent Schools Act*, Group 1 accredited Islamic schools receive 50% per-pupil operating grants from the Ministry of Education, resulting in significantly lower parent fees than in Ontario.
