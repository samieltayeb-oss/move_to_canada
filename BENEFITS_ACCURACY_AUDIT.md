# GOVERNMENT BENEFITS ACCURACY & STATUTORY AUDIT

**Project:** move_to_canada (V1 Alberta)  
**Regulatory Framework:** Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), Health Canada, Government of Alberta  
**Benefit Cycle:** July 2026 – June 2027  
**Family Demographics:** 2 Adults (Yassir & Spouse), 3 Children (Ages 16, 11, 5)

---

## 1. Statutory Benefit Review

### 1.1 Canada Child Benefit (CCB)
- **Authority:** Canada Revenue Agency (CRA T4114 Guidelines).
- **Payment Frequency:** Monthly (20th of each month).
- **2026–2027 Benefit Rates:**
  - Child Under 6 (Age 5): **$8,157.00 CAD / yr** ($679.75 / mo).
  - Child 6–17 (Ages 16, 11): **$6,883.00 CAD / yr** ($573.58 / mo per child).
  - **Maximum Gross Potential (3 Children):** **$21,923.00 CAD / yr** ($1,826.92 / mo).
- **Clawback Reductions (3 Children Formula):**
  - First Threshold: **$38,237.00 CAD** (Reduction rate: 19.0%).
  - Second Threshold: **$79,349.00 CAD** (Indexed from prior base; reduction rate: 8.0% + $7,811.28).
- **Newcomer Intake Documentation:** Form RC66 (*Canada Child Benefits Application*) and Form RC66SCH (*Status in Canada & Statement of World Income*).
- **Statutory Finding:** Pre-arrival Saudi Arabian worldwide income determines Year 1 CCB payments (from date of arrival until June 30 of the following year).

---

### 1.2 Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB)
- **Historical Context:** Formally renamed and expanded by 25% from the former GST/HST Credit starting July 2026.
- **Payment Frequency:** Quarterly (July 5, October 5, January 5, April 5).
- **2026 Formula (Couple with 3 Children):**
  - Couple Base: **$890.00 CAD**
  - Per Child: **$234.00 CAD**
  - 3 Children: `$234 * 3 = $702.00 CAD`
  - Maximum Potential: `$890 + $702 = \mathbf{\$1,592.00\text{ CAD / yr}}\;(\mathbf{\$398.00\text{ / quarter}})$.
  - Clawback Threshold: **$44,324.00 CAD** AFNI (5% phaseout rate).
  - Full Phaseout Zero-Point: **$76,164.00 CAD**.

---

### 1.3 Alberta Child and Family Benefit (ACFB)
- **Authority:** Alberta Treasury Board and Finance / Administered by CRA.
- **Payment Frequency:** 4 Quarterly Installments (August 27, November 27, February 27, May 27).
- **2026 Formula (3 Children):**
  - **Base Component:** Up to **$3,057.00 CAD / yr** (Phaseout begins at $27,024 AFNI at 7.5% rate; zero at $67,784).
  - **Working Component:** Up to **$1,920.00 CAD / yr** (Phases in above $2,760 working income at 17.9%; phaseout begins at $45,285 at 13% rate; zero at $60,054).
  - **Combined Maximum:** **$4,977.00 CAD / yr** ($1,244.25 / quarter).

---

### 1.4 Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan (AHCIP)
- **Coverage Effective Date:** **Day 1 of establishing Alberta residency** (Zero-day wait period for international arrivals with PR status).
- **Mandatory 3-Month Window:** Application must be lodged within 3 months of landing; otherwise retroactive coverage to arrival date is forfeited.
- **Covered:** Physician care, hospital surgeries, diagnostic imaging/labs, child eye exams (ages 0–18).
- **Excluded:** Adult routine dental, outpatient pharmacy drugs, adult vision, ambulance transport ($250 without transport, $385 with transport).

---

### 1.5 Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)
- **Eligibility:** No access to private/employer dental insurance, tax return filed, and AFNI strictly under $90,000.
- **Copay Brackets:**
  - AFNI < $70,000: 0% copay.
  - AFNI $70,000–$79,999: 40% copay.
  - AFNI $80,000–$89,999: 60% copay.
  - AFNI $90,000+: Ineligible (100% out of pocket).
- **Balance Billing Alert:** Alberta dentists are not bound by the CDCP fee schedule; patients are liable for fees above CDCP limits.

---

### 1.6 Alberta Child Health Benefit (ACHB)
- **Income Ceiling for Couple + 3 Children:** Family net income strictly below **$41,594.00 CAD**.
- **Coverage:** 100% basic child dental, 1 pair of eyeglasses per child per year, essential prescription drugs, emergency ambulance.

---

### 1.7 Alberta Blue Cross Non-Group Coverage (Plan 118)
- **Monthly Premium:** **$118.00 CAD / mo** (family standard) or **$82.60 CAD / mo** (subsidized).
- **Prescription Drug Benefit:** 30% copay up to a maximum cap of **$35.00 CAD per prescription**.

---

### 1.8 Alberta $15/Day Child Care Subsidy
- Applies to Child 3 (Age 5, enrolled in licensed full-time daycare/preschool).
- Parent Fee: **~$315.00 CAD / mo** vs. $1,250 market rate (saves **$935.00 CAD / mo**).

---

### 1.9 Education Savings (RESP / CESG / CLB)
- **Basic CESG:** 20% federal match on the first $2,500 contributed per child per year ($500/year grant; lifetime max $7,200 per child).
- **Canada Learning Bond (CLB):** Up to $2,000 per child for modest-income families with $0 personal contribution required.
