# BRITISH COLUMBIA TAX ENGINE SPECIFICATION (2026)

## 1. Statutory Brackets & Rates (Budget 2026 Adjusted)
- **Lowest Rate:** 5.60% up to $50,363
- **Second Bracket:** 7.70% from $50,363.01 to $100,728
- **Third Bracket:** 10.50% from $100,728.01 to $115,648
- **Fourth Bracket:** 12.29% from $115,648.01 to $140,430
- **Fifth Bracket:** 14.70% from $140,430.01 to $190,405
- **Sixth Bracket:** 16.80% from $190,405.01 to $265,545
- **Top Bracket:** 20.50% over $265,545

## 2. Basic Personal Amount Credit
- BPA: $13,216 CAD
- Credit = $13,216 * 5.60% = $740.10 CAD

## 3. BC Tax Reduction Credit
- Max $690, phasing out at 3.56% of net income over $25,570.
- Completely phased out at $44,952. For all benchmarks >= $60k, credit = $0.

## 4. Provincial Surtaxes & Premiums
- BC has **no surtaxes** and **no individual MSP premiums** ($0).
