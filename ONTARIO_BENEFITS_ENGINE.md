# ONTARIO BENEFITS ENGINE SPECIFICATION (2026)

## 1. Ontario Child Benefit (OCB)
- **Authority:** Ontario Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services / Canada Revenue Agency
- **Maximum Annual Amount:** $1,760 CAD / child ($146.66 / mo per child under 18)
- **Phaseout Threshold:** Adjusted Family Net Income (AFNI) of $26,865 CAD
- **Clawback Rate:** 8.0% of AFNI above $26,865 CAD
- **Family of 5 (3 Children) Example:**
  - Base Maximum: $1,760 * 3 = $5,280 CAD / yr ($440.00 / mo)
  - At $91,900 CAD income (Yassir Year 1 world income): $5,280 - (($91,900 - $26,865) * 0.08) = $5,280 - $5,202.80 = **$77.20 CAD / yr** ($6.43 / mo)
  - At $125,000 CAD income: Full phaseout ($0 CAD)

## 2. Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)
- **Authority:** Ontario Ministry of Finance / CRA
- **Components:**
  - Ontario Sales Tax Credit (OSTC): Up to $378/adult + $378/child
  - Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit (OEPTC): Up to $1,307 for non-senior renters (claiming 20% of rent paid)
  - Phaseout: Fully phases out above ~$45,000 to $60,000 for family of 5

## 3. CWELCC Childcare Subsidy
- **Status in 2026:** Parent fee capped at $22/day for licensed care for children under 6.
