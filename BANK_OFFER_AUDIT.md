## 2. Institutional Analysis & Unique Selling Propositions

### 1. ATB Financial (Alberta Provincial Champion)
- **100% Alberta Government Deposit Guarantee:** Unlike CDIC which caps deposit insurance at $100,000 per category, ATB is backed by the Government of Alberta without limit.
- **Mortgage & Foreign Underwriting:** Recognizes Saudi Arabian credit and employment history for residential mortgages without prohibitive surcharge penalties.

### 2. RBC Royal Bank (Credit Limit Leader)
- **$15,000 Credit Card Limit:** Highest unconditional newcomer credit limit without requiring Canadian credit history or cash collateral.

### 3. TD Bank (Highest Pure Cash Bonus)
- **$500.00 Liquid Cash Deposit:** Generous upfront cash payment deposited into chequing upon setting up direct deposit and two recurring bill payments.

### 4. CIBC (Best Longevity & Remittances)
- **24-Month Fee Waiver:** Two full years of waived monthly chequing fees ($406.80 cumulative savings).
- **Direct Transfers to Saudi Arabia:** $0 transfer fees to Saudi bank accounts via CIBC Global Money Transfer.

## 3. The Credit Limit Education Mandate
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A $15,000 Credit Limit is Credit, Not Free Money.**  
> Carrying an unpaid balance triggers interest rates of **20.99% to 22.99% APR**. The module educates newcomers to keep monthly utilization below 25%–30% and set up automatic monthly statement autopay.
