# JOB OPPORTUNITY MATCH SCORING METHODOLOGY
**System:** Profile Match Score Algorithm  
**Model Type:** Multi-Attribute Heuristic Alignment Engine  
**Version:** September 2026  

---

## 1. Score Transparency Principle
The Profile Match Score is an explainable heuristic measure of **documentary and experiential alignment**. It is explicitly **NOT** a probability of hiring or an interview guarantee.

---

## 2. Factor Weights & Mathematical Framework

$$\text{Profile Match Score} = \sum_{i=1}^{8} (w_i \times s_i)$$

Where $\sum w_i = 100$:

| Factor $i$ | Factor Name | Weight $w_i$ | Scoring Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Functional Domain Similarity** | 25% | Overlap between capital markets / fund operations and target Canadian job duties. |
| 2 | **Quantified Technical Skills** | 20% | Financial modeling, NAV valuation, trade settlement, custodian reconciliation. |
| 3 | **Seniority Alignment** | 15% | Years of professional experience matching job level (Analyst vs Manager vs Director). |
| 4 | **Regulated Industry Experience** | 15% | Prior experience in fiduciary, Capital Market Authority (CMA), or banking compliance. |
| 5 | **Canadian Certification Readiness** | 10% | Feasibility of obtaining required Canadian credentials (e.g. CSC in 60 days). |
| 6 | **Language & Communication** | 5% | Fluent bilingual business English capability. |
| 7 | **Geographic Mobility** | 5% | Confirmed permanent relocation intent to Calgary, AB. |
| 8 | **Work Authorization** | 5% | Permanent Resident (PR) or open work permit status. |

---

## 3. Fit Classification Thresholds
- **$\ge 85\%$ — Strong Match:** High technical and operational alignment. Target for immediate customized application and recruiter outreach.
- **$70\% - 84\%$ — Possible Match:** Transferable core competencies with manageable Canadian skill gap (e.g. Canadian Securities Course needed).
- **$55\% - 69\%$ — Stretch Role:** Requires substantial bridging coursework or additional Canadian industry certifications.
- **$< 55\%$ — Low Match:** Different occupational discipline; not recommended for initial 90-day focus.
