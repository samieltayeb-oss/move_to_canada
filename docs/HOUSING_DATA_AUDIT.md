## 1. CMHC vs. Rentals.ca Benchmark Comparison

| Metric | CMHC Purpose-Built Survey (Calgary CMA) | Rentals.ca / Urbanation Open-Market Asking Rent | Key Methodological Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Scope** | Occupied units across multi-unit buildings (3+ units). Includes long-standing sitting leases. | Actively advertised vacant units on open market. | CMHC reflects current living rent; Rentals.ca reflects immediate cost to enter the market. |
| **Vacancy Rate** | **5.0% – 5.1%** (Recovered from 1.4% in 2023 due to record completions). | Inventory supply index up 28% y/y across apartment stock. | High completion pipeline easing apartment market; detached single-family remains tight. |
| **2-Bedroom Average** | **$1,908 / month** | **$2,100 – $2,180 / month** | ~$200 turnover premium for newly signed leases. |
| **3-Bedroom Average** | **$2,118 – $2,175 / month** (all occupied) | **$2,326 – $2,371 / month** (open asking rent) | 3-bedroom units show strong demand from growing families. |
| **4-Bedroom Detached** | Not tracked separately in primary purpose-built table. | **$2,600 – $3,300 / month** (Median: $2,697; Avg: $2,950) | Detached houses are virtually 100% secondary market (private landlords). |

## 2. Neighbourhood Quadrant Profiles for Yassir's Family (2 Adults, 3 Kids)

### A. Northeast (NE): Saddleridge, Taradale, Skyview Ranch, Cityscape
* **Housing Stock:** Modern 2-storey laned houses, front-attached garage homes, and duplexes.
* **Price Benchmark:** 3-bed townhouse: $2,100–$2,400; 4-bed detached home: $2,500–$3,100.
* **Islamic Infrastructure:** Immediate access (<5–10 min) to Akram Jomaa Islamic Centre (MCC) and Calgary Islamic School (CIS).
* **Transit:** Saddletowne Station (Blue Line CTrain) connects directly to Downtown in 38 mins.
* **Commercial:** Basha Foods, Asian Halal Meat, FreshCo Saddletowne, Genesis Centre YMCA.
* **Catchment Alert:** Saddleridge elementary schools face high capacity; register early through CBE Welcome Centre to prevent overflow to neighbouring communities.

### B. Northwest (NW): Panorama Hills, Evanston, Nolan Hill, Sage Hill, Carrington
* **Housing Stock:** Newer master-planned suburban homes built 2010–2024, walking paths, ravines.
* **Price Benchmark:** 3-bed townhouse/duplex: $2,400–$2,700; 4-bed detached home: $2,800–$3,400.
* **Islamic Infrastructure:** 10–15 mins to ICNC Ranchlands Mosque & MAC Al-Salam Centre; 15 mins via Stoney Trail ring road to Akram Jomaa.
* **Transit:** Route 301 BRT to Downtown; Stoney Trail vehicle corridor.
* **Commercial:** Real Canadian Superstore Country Hills (major certified halal section), Sage Hill Crossing.
* **Catchment Alert:** Evanston has experienced CBE lotteries in recent cycles; verify exact designated school before signing lease.

### C. Southwest (SW): West Springs, Aspen Woods, Cougar Ridge
* **Housing Stock:** Executive detached homes, luxury villas, mature ravines.
* **Price Benchmark:** 3-bed executive townhouse: $2,900–$3,400; 4-bed detached estate: $3,500–$4,500+.
* **Islamic Infrastructure:** 7–10 mins to Calgary Islamic Centre (SW Masjid / CICSW - 5615 14 Ave SW).
* **Transit:** 69th Street CTrain Station (18–22 mins direct to Downtown).
* **Schools:** Premier public (Ernest Manning) and private academies (Weber Academy, Rundle College, CFIS).
* **Trade-off:** Premium lifestyle and shortest commute, but highest rent burden in Calgary.

### D. Southeast (SE): Mahogany, Auburn Bay
* **Housing Stock:** Lake-community detached homes, front-garage properties with lake privileges.
* **Price Benchmark:** 3-bed: $2,500–$2,850; 4-bed: $2,900–$3,500.
* **Islamic Infrastructure:** Musallas in Queensland & Legacy; 30+ min highway drive to Akram Jomaa.
* **Amenities:** Private 43- to 63-acre freshwater lakes, Seton Urban District, world's largest YMCA.
* **Trade-off:** Outstanding family recreation, but greatest distance from central Muslim community facilities and longest downtown commute (45–60 mins).
