## 1. Major Mosques & Islamic Centres

### 1. Akram Jomaa Islamic Centre (Muslim Council of Calgary - MCC)
- **Location:** 2624 – 39 Avenue NE, Calgary, AB T1Y 5V7
- **Coordinates & Quadrant:** Northeast (NE) Calgary
- **Capacity & Structure:** Main prayer hall accommodates 3,000+ worshippers; extensive sisters' mezzanine, youth gymnasium, banquet hall.
- **Congregational Jumu'ah:** 3 separate shifts year-round (approx. 12:30 PM, 1:30 PM, 2:30 PM seasonal).
- **Core Programs:** Tahfeez al-Qur'an academy, evening Hifz classes, weekend Arabic/Islamic madrasah, MCC Youth Council, food bank, and centralized Islamic funeral mortuary service (Calgary Islamic Funeral Services).
- **Ramadan & Eid:** Daily community Iftars serving 1,000+ meals nightly; full 20-rak'ah Taraweeh; multiple Eid shifts + massive community prayer gathering at Genesis Centre or BMO Centre (25,000+ Muslims).

### 2. Downtown Calgary Mosque (Islamic Information Society of Calgary - IISC)
- **Location:** Unit 200, 1009 – 7th Avenue SW, Calgary, AB T2P 1A8
- **Quadrant:** Downtown Core (West End, right along 7th Ave CTrain Free Fare Zone).
- **Primary Function:** Accessible prayer facility for corporate professionals in Downtown Calgary.
- **Jumu'ah:** 2 shifts tailored to corporate lunch breaks (12:30 PM and 1:30 PM).
- **Affiliated Youth Centre:** IISC Al-Kahf Youth Centre (4128 6 St NE), offering youth drop-in lounges, academic tutoring, and halaqas.

### 3. Al-Salam Centre (Muslim Association of Canada - MAC)
- **Location:** 6415 Ranchview Drive NW, Calgary, AB T3G 1B5 (Ranchlands)
- **Quadrant:** Northwest (NW) Calgary
- **Programs:** 2 Friday Jumu'ah shifts, MAC Islamic Preschool & Kindergarten, Al-Salam Weekend School for Quran & Arabic, MAC Youth sports leagues, summer and winter youth camps.

### 4. Calgary Islamic Centre (SW Masjid / CICSW)
- **Location:** 5615 – 14 Avenue SW, Calgary, AB T3H 2E8 (Westgate/Wildwood)
- **Quadrant:** Southwest (SW) Calgary
- **Programs:** 2 Jumu'ah shifts, Nibras Quran & Tajweed School for youth, adult Arabic immersion, certified Islamic marriage (Nikah) officiation, family mediation.

### 5. South Calgary Musallas & Future Masjid Project (ICSC)
- **Locations:** 539 Queensland Drive SE & Legacy Musalla (180 Legacy Main St SE #110).
- **Expansion:** Land acquired at 16555 – 104 Street SE for the multi-million dollar South Calgary Islamic Centre to serve Seton, Cranston, Auburn Bay, and Mahogany.

## 2. Accredited Islamic Schools

### 1. Calgary Islamic School (CIS) — Prairie Rose Public Schools
- **Campuses:**
  * **Akram Jomaa Campus:** 2612 37 Ave NE (K to Grade 12)
  * **Omar Bin Al-Khattab Campus:** 225 28 St SE (K to Grade 9)
- **Governance:** Public alternative faith-based program funded by the Alberta Ministry of Education in partnership with the Calgary Islamic School Society.
- **Curriculum:** 100% Alberta Program of Studies + daily Arabic language, Islamic studies, daily congregational Dhuhr/Asr prayer, and specialized Tahfeez stream.
- **Approved 2026–2027 Fees:**
  * Kindergarten to Grade 9: **$2,450 / year** (Sibling rate: $2,250)
  * Grades 10 to 12 & Tahfeez: **$2,690 / year** (Sibling rate: $2,490)
  * Yellow school bus transportation available across NE, NW, SE, SW (~$1,200–$1,500/yr).
- **Waitlist Alert:** Heavy waitlists. Priority granted to returning students and siblings; prospective testing opens annually in mid-March.

### 2. Al-Amal Academy
- **Location:** 9 Royal Vista Drive NW, Calgary, AB T3R 0N2
- **Quadrant:** Northwest (NW)
- **Governance:** Accredited alternative school with Prairie Land Public Schools and Pen and Paper Foundation.
- **Grades:** Kindergarten to Grade 9.
- **Distinctives:** Singapore Math, Project REACH character development, intensive Quran & Arabic.

## 3. Halal Groceries & Meat Supply Chain

1. **Basha Foods International (2717 Sunridge Way NE):** 30,000+ sq ft international market with in-house halal butcher (lamb, beef, goat, poultry), Middle Eastern bakery, imported Syrian, Lebanese, and Saudi dry goods.
2. **Asian Halal Meat & Food (55 Westwinds Cres NE):** Custom whole-animal hand-slaughtered (Zabiha) halal butcher.
3. **Chalo! FreshCo & Westgate Halal (Saddletowne Circle NE):** Full-service supermarket with dedicated fresh halal butcher counter.
4. **Mainstream Supermarkets:** Real Canadian Superstore (Westwinds, Country Hills, Sunridge) and Walmart Supercentres stock Mina (HMA/HMCA certified) and Sufra halal meats.
5. **Costco Business Centre (2853 32 St NE):** Bulk whole halal lamb/goat carcasses, primal cuts, and cases of halal poultry for large family households.
