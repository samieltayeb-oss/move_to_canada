## 1. Macro Economic Landscape in Calgary
Calgary is Canada's second-largest corporate concentration hub after Toronto, with the highest concentration of corporate headquarters per capita in the nation. While Toronto dominates broad capital markets, Calgary's financial sector is defined by:
1. **Commercial Banking & Syndicated Lending:** Massive demand for corporate credit analysts underwriting mid-market commercial loans in logistics, agriculture, and energy services.
2. **Institutional Asset Management:** Headquartered institutional giants (**Mawer Investment Management** with $80B+ AUM; **AIMCo**'s Calgary office managing sovereign pensions; **ATB Capital Markets**).
3. **Corporate Treasuries & Energy Trading:** Energy multinationals (**Enbridge, TC Energy, Cenovus, Suncor**) operate corporate finance floors with annual liquidity volumes matching mid-size commercial banks.
4. **Emerging Fintech:** Calgary's East Village innovation district hosts **Neo Financial** (700+ employees, digital banking HQ), **Benevity** (global corporate social responsibility software), and **Helcim** (payments infrastructure).

## 2. Target NOC Occupational Profiles & Compensation

### NOC 11101 — Financial and Investment Analysts (TEER 1)
- **Job Bank Outlook (Alberta):** Good / High Demand.
- **Calgary Base Salary Range (Robert Half 2026):**
  - 25th Percentile: **$68,000 CAD**
  - 50th Percentile (Median): **$88,500 CAD**
  - 75th Percentile: **$112,000 CAD**
- **Core Skill Competencies:** Portfolio valuation, NAV reconciliation, financial modeling, financial statement stress-testing.

### NOC 10010 — Financial Managers / Investment Operations Managers (TEER 0)
- **Job Bank Outlook (Alberta):** Good / Stable Demand.
- **Calgary Base Salary Range:**
  - 25th Percentile: **$105,000 CAD**
  - 50th Percentile (Median): **$132,000 CAD**
  - 75th Percentile: **$168,000+ CAD**
- **Core Skill Competencies:** Trade lifecycle settlement, custodian clearing reconciliation, operational risk mitigation, OSFI compliance.

### NOC 11102 — Financial Underwriters / Commercial Credit Analysts (TEER 1)
- **Job Bank Outlook (Alberta):** Very Strong.
- **Calgary Base Salary Range:**
  - 25th Percentile: **$82,000 CAD**
  - 50th Percentile (Median): **$105,000 CAD**
  - 75th Percentile: **$135,000 CAD**
