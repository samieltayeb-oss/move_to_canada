## 2. In-Store vs. Delivery Markup Disclosure
- **Costco.ca / Same-Day via Instacart:** Marked up 15%–20% over physical warehouse prices. In-person shopping is strongly recommended for Costco savings.
- **PC Express (Superstore):** Uses in-store flyer pricing for click-and-collect pickup, saving 2 hours of weekly grocery time.
