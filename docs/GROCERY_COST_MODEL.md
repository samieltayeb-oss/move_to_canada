# CALGARY FAMILY GROCERY COST MODEL
**Target Household:** 5 Persons (2 Adults: Male 38, Female 36; 3 Children: Ages 11, 8, 4)

## 2. Alberta Regional & Halal Premium Adjustments
1. **Alberta Food Price Inflation Factor (+4%):** Alberta is projected to experience slightly above-average food inflation due to provincial transportation and logistics inputs.
2. **Halal Fresh Meat Premium (+6% net basket impact):** Sourcing certified hand-slaughtered Zabiha halal poultry, beef, and lamb typically costs 10%–15% more than conventional uncertified bulk commodity meats.

$$\text{Adjusted Annual Benchmark} = \$16,960 \times 1.04 \times 1.06 \approx \mathbf{\$18,696\text{ CAD/yr}}\;(\mathbf{\$1,558\text{ CAD/mo}})$$

## 3. The Three Calgary Grocery Lifestyles
- **Value Shopper ($1,310/mo / $302/wk):** Costco wholesale staples (rice, flour, oil, eggs, paper goods) + Superstore/Walmart weekly specials + basic halal poultry.
- **Balanced Family ($1,630/mo / $376/wk):** Standard executive baseline: high fresh fruit and berries, premium halal beef/lamb cuts from local butchers (Basha / Saned), school snack packs.
- **Premium Organic ($2,080/mo / $480/wk):** Extensive organic produce, specialty Middle Eastern imported pantry items, artisanal olive oils, prepared convenience meals.
