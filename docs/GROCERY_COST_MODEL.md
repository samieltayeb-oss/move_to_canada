## 2. Alberta Regional & Halal Premium Adjustments
1. **Alberta Food Price Inflation Factor (+4%):** Alberta is projected to experience slightly above-average food inflation due to provincial transportation and inter-provincial logistics inputs.
2. **Halal Fresh Meat Premium (+6% net basket impact):** Sourcing certified hand-slaughtered Zabiha halal poultry, beef, and lamb typically costs 10%–15% more than conventional uncertified bulk commodity meats.

$$\text{Adjusted Annual Benchmark} = \$19,780 \times 1.04 \times 1.06 \approx \mathbf{\$21,996\text{ CAD/yr}}\;(\mathbf{\$1,833\text{ CAD/mo}})$$

## 3. The Three Calgary Grocery Lifestyles
- **Value Shopper ($1,474/mo / $340/wk):** Costco wholesale staples (rice, flour, oil, eggs, paper goods) + Superstore/Walmart weekly specials + bulk halal chicken.
- **Balanced Family ($1,833/mo / $423/wk):** Standard executive baseline: high fresh fruit and berries, premium halal beef/lamb cuts from local butchers (Basha / Saned), school snack packs.
- **Premium Organic ($2,336/mo / $539/wk):** Extensive organic produce, specialty Middle Eastern imported pantry items, artisanal olive oils, prepared convenience meals.

## 4. Costco Wholesale Economics (September 2026 Verified Fees)
- **Standard Gold Star Membership:** $65.00 CAD / yr
- **Executive 2% Cash-Back Membership:** $130.00 CAD / yr
- **Upgrade Delta:** $65.00 CAD / yr
- **Marginal Upgrade Break-Even:** Spend of **$270.83 / mo** ($3,250 / yr) yields $65 cash-back, fully covering the upgrade cost over Gold Star.
- **Full Zero-Cost Membership Break-Even:** Spend of **$541.67 / mo** ($6,500 / yr) yields $130 cash-back, paying for the entire Executive membership with zero net annual fee.
- **Yassir Family Recommendation:** At an expected Costco spend of ~$800/mo, 2% cash-back generates **~$192/yr**, covering the entire $130 membership and yielding a net profit of **+$62/yr**, in addition to ~$250–$350/yr in fuel savings at the Calgary East Hills / Sunridge Costco Gas bars.
