# Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) Calculation Specification

## 1. Statutory Background & 2026 Transition
Effective July 2026, the federal government officially transformed and expanded the former **Goods and Services Tax / Harmonized Sales Tax (GST/HST) Credit** into the:

# Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB)

### Critical Terminology Rule
All system references must utilize the modern **CGEB** naming convention. The term "GST/HST Credit" is retained strictly for historical and transitional explanation.

---

## 2. 2026–2027 Benefit Components & Maximums

| Component | Statutory Annual Amount CAD | Payout Schedule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eligible Married / Common-Law Couple Base** | $\$890.00$ CAD | 4 Equal Quarterly Payments |
| **Each Eligible Dependent Child (Under 19 Years)** | $\$234.00$ CAD per child | 4 Equal Quarterly Payments |
| **Three (3) Children Total** | $\$702.00$ CAD | 4 Equal Quarterly Payments |
| **Theoretical Maximum (Couple + 3 Children)** | **$\$1,592.00$ CAD / year** | **$\$398.00$ CAD / quarter** |

## 3. Phaseout Mechanics & Income Threshold
The CGEB is designed to offset consumption taxes for low-to-modest income families.

* **Base Income Threshold:** **$\$44,324.00$ CAD** Adjusted Family Net Income (AFNI).
* **Clawback Rate:** **5.0%** of family net income in excess of $\$44,324$.

$$\text{CGEB Reduction} = \max\left(0, (\text{AFNI} - \$44,324) \times 0.05\right)$$
$$\text{Net Annual CGEB} = \max\left(0, \$1,592 - \text{CGEB Reduction}\right)$$

### Complete Phaseout Point for Family of 5
$$\text{Complete Phaseout AFNI} = \$44,324 + \left(\frac{\$1,592}{0.05}\right) = \mathbf{\$76,164.00\text{ CAD}}$$

### Application to Yassir's Relocation
* If Yassir’s household income during the first transition months reflects low Canadian taxable income, the family may receive the full **$\$398.00$ CAD quarterly** deposit.
* Once household earnings stabilize at the professional IT benchmark of $\$125,000$ CAD, the benefit phases out completely to **$\$0$**.

2. **October 5**

4. **April 5**
