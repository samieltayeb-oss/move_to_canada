### Stream 1: Federal & Alberta Settlement
- **Lead Subagent:** Settlement & Legal Specialist (`a02e7920-99ae-41af-8005-5e14146124c1`)
- **Key Findings:**
  - AHCIP has **0-day waiting period** for international newcomers moving directly to Alberta. Must register in person at an Alberta Registry with passport, COPR/Work Permit, and lease.
  - AHCIP covers 100% medically necessary physician visits and hospital care, but excludes outpatient prescription drugs, adult dental, adult eye exams, and ambulance co-pays ($250–$385).

  - GDL Modernization (April 1, 2023) eliminated the advanced road test; applicants with 2+ years verified foreign experience can test directly for a full Class 5 (Non-GDL) licence.
  - Bringing modern vehicles under 15 years old from Saudi Arabia is strictly prohibited under Transport Canada CMVSS rules; strongly advised to sell in KSA.
