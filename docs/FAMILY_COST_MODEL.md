### Strategic Guidance on Cash Transfers
1. **Reporting Limits at Canadian Border:** Under CBSA regulations, there is **no limit** on the total amount of funds a newcomer can bring into Canada. However, any amount equal to or exceeding **$10,000 CAD** (or foreign equivalent) must be truthfully declared on CBSA Form **E677**. Failure to declare can result in seizure and penalties.
2. **Wire Timing:** Use bank-to-bank SWIFT wire transfers or reputable international currency brokers (OFX, Wise, CIBC Global Money Transfer) directly to the Canadian newcomer account once opened in branch to avoid retail counter FX spreads.
