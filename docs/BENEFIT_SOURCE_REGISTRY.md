# Canadian Government Benefits & Bank Intelligence Source Registry

## 1. Statutory Hierarchy of Authority
Information within this command center adheres strictly to primary-source documentation:
1. **Tier 1 (Statutory & Government Portals):** Acts of Parliament, Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), Service Canada, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), and Government of Alberta departments.
2. **Tier 2 (Official Financial Institutions):** Primary product disclosures, fee schedules, and legal terms published directly by Canadian Schedule I chartered banks and ATB Financial.
3. **Excluded as Primary Truth:** Comparison blogs, aggregate lead-generation websites, and third-party sponsored articles.

## 3. Maintenance Protocols & Expiry Automation
* Bank offers marked with fixed calendar expirations automatically flag for review in the system upon reaching day 30.
* CCB, CGEB, and ACFB calculations are scheduled for annual review every June prior to the new July benefit year cycle.
