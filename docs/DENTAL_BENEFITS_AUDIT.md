# Dental Benefits & Oral Health Coverage Audit

## 1. The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) Architecture
The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) is a federally funded program administered by Health Canada with Sun Life Canada serving as the contracted claims processor.

### Strict Statutory Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for CDCP coverage, an applicant must satisfy **all four (4)** criteria simultaneously:
1. **No Access to Dental Insurance:** Must have no access to employer-sponsored dental benefits, pension dental plans, or family group coverage.  
   *(Crucial Note: Choosing to opt out of an employer dental plan does not restore eligibility; the mere availability of employer dental insurance disqualifies the family).*
2. **Adjusted Family Net Income (AFNI):** Must be strictly below **$\$90,000.00$ CAD**.
3. **Tax Resident Status:** Must be a Canadian resident for tax purposes.
4. **Tax Return on File:** Must have filed a Canadian personal tax return for the previous tax year (co-ordinated through CRA).

## 3. The Dentist "Balance Billing" Reality in Alberta
A primary newcomer trap is assuming that a "0% Co-pay" rating equals completely free dental care.

### The Problem:
* The CDCP establishes its own standardized national fee schedule, which is significantly lower than the **Alberta Dental Association (ADA) Fee Guide**.
* Alberta dentists are **not** legally bound to accept the CDCP rate as full payment.
* If a Calgary dentist charges according to the Alberta fee guide ($e.g., \$320$ for a cleaning and checkup) and the CDCP only establishes $\$240$, the patient is billed the **$\$80$ difference (balance billing)** out of pocket, even with a 0% copay certificate.

### Recommended Patient Action:
Always inquire with the dental office prior to booking:  
*"Do you accept CDCP assignment of benefits, and do you balance bill above the CDCP established fee schedule?"*

## 4. Alberta Child Health Benefit (ACHB) Safety Net
For lower-income Alberta families, the provincial **Alberta Child Health Benefit (ACHB)** provides comprehensive dental and vision care for children:

* **Eligibility Threshold for Couple with 3 Children:** Family net income strictly below **$\$41,594.00$ CAD**.
* **Child Dental Benefit:** Covers 100% of basic dental services (annual checkup, cleanings, dental x-rays, fillings, extractions) for children up to age 18.
* **Child Vision & Prescription Benefit:** One complete pair of prescription eyeglasses per year, plus 100% of prescription medications on the Alberta Drug Benefit List.
