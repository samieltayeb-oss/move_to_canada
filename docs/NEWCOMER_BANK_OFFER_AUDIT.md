# Newcomer Bank Offer Audit (Big 5 + ATB Financial)

## 1. Executive Methodology & Reality-Check Principle
Canadian chartered banks heavily market newcomer packages with aggregate headlines advertising "Up to $1,800 to $2,300 in Value."

In reality, these advertised numbers conflate **tangible cash** with:
* Reward and movie points (Scene+, Avion) that expire or require extensive discretionary retail spending.
* Trading commission credits for direct brokerage accounts that newcomers rarely execute in Year 1.
* Credit card annual fee waivers that simply waive the first year's premium on an upscale credit card.

### The Real Cash Standard:
This audit isolates **Pure Cash Deposits** and **Real Chequing Fee Waivers** from marketing bonuses to evaluate actual bottom-line value for Yassir’s family.

## 3. Institution-Specific Highlights & Operational Nuances

### 1. ATB Financial (Alberta's Provincial Powerhouse)
* **The Sovereign Guarantee:** Unlike the Big 5 banks which are insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC) up to $\$100,000$ per category, ATB is a provincial Crown corporation where **100% of all customer deposits are unconditionally guaranteed by the Government of Alberta**.
* **Mortgage Advantage:** ATB's underwriting department is headquartered in Alberta; loan officers accept overseas Saudi Arabian banking statements and previous employment history without demanding punitive interest rate surcharges.
* **Youth Accounts:** The *ATB Generation Account* provides free mobile banking and debit cards for children ages 0 to 18 (ideal for all 3 kids: 16, 11, and 5).

### 2. RBC Royal Bank (Credit Limit Champion)
* **Credit Card Access:** Unrivaled newcomer credit limit approvals up to **$\$15,000.00$ CAD** on the RBC Cash Back Mastercard or RBC Avion Visa without prior Canadian credit history.
* **Network:** Largest physical footprint in Calgary with 48 retail branches and widespread multi-lingual customer service.

### 3. TD Bank (Highest Immediate Cash Incentive)
* **Cash Offer:** Generous **$\$500.00$ CAD pure cash deposit** when completing an Unlimited Chequing Account setup, recurring direct deposit, and qualifying bill payment.
* **Customer Convenience:** Calgary branches maintain extended hours, remaining open until 8:00 PM on Thursdays/Fridays and operating on Sundays in major shopping malls.

### 4. CIBC (Best Longevity & Remittance)
* **2-Year Fee Waiver:** While other institutions revert to charging $\$11.95–\$17.95/month after 12 months, CIBC provides **two full years (24 months)** of zero account maintenance fees.
* **Remittances to Saudi Arabia:** *CIBC Global Money Transfer (GMT)* facilitates direct transfers to Saudi Arabian bank accounts in Saudi Riyals (SAR) with zero wire fees.

### 5. Scotiabank (Automotive Financing Dominance)
* **Car Purchase Support:** Scotiabank is Canada's largest automotive dealership lender. The *StartRight Auto Finance* program provides straightforward financing on family vehicles (Toyota Highlander, Honda Pilot) without requiring 2 years of local credit history.

## 4. The Credit Limit Education Rule
> [!WARNING]
> **A $15,000 Credit Limit is Credit, Not Free Money.**  
> A newcomer credit card limit must be treated strictly as a transactional mechanism to build credit score. Carrying an unpaid balance incurs statutory interest charges of **20.99% to 22.99% APR**. Newcomers should set up automatic bill payment from their chequing account for 100% of the statement balance each month.
