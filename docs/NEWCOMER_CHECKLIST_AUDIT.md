# NEWCOMER GOVERNMENT & SETTLEMENT CHECKLIST AUDIT
**Project:** MOVE_TO_CANADA — Yassir's Calgary Move Command Center  
**Applicability:** Permanent Resident (PR) & Work Permit Pathways  
**Statutory Basis:** IRCC, CRA, Service Canada, Service Alberta, Calgary Board of Education  

---

## Chronological Task Matrix & Statutory Verification

### 1. Pre-Arrival Phase (30–90 Days Before Landing)
- [ ] **IRCC Free Pre-Arrival Services:** Register with federally funded settlement orientation agencies (Planning for Canada / S.U.C.C.E.S.S.) using Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR) or Work Permit approval letter.
- [ ] **Saudi Driving Documentation:** Obtain official Saudi Traffic Police (Moroor) driving abstract/record with digital QR validation from Absher/Mojaz. Secure certified English translation through an accredited translator.
- [ ] **Auto Insurance Experience Letter:** Secure official claims-free driving history certificate from your Saudi motor insurer (Najm / Tawuniya) on official letterhead stating continuous insured years with zero at-fault claims (critical for auto insurance rating in Alberta).
- [ ] **Children's School & Immunization Records:** Compile official school transcripts, previous report cards, and certified English translated immunization records for all three children.
- [ ] **Remote Canadian Bank Account Opening:** Initiate newcomer account application via RBC, TD, Scotiabank, CIBC, or BMO to enable wire transfers prior to flight.
- [ ] **Temporary Accommodation:** Book 3–4 weeks of furnished temporary lodging (Airbnb or executive suite) with high proximity to transit and amenities.
- [ ] **Vehicle Import Verification:** Confirm that modern Saudi-market family vehicle will NOT be shipped, as non-US vehicles under 15 years old are inadmissible under Transport Canada MVSA regulations.

### 2. Landing Day (Port of Entry — Calgary YYC / Toronto YYZ / Montreal YUL)
- [ ] **CBSA Border Processing:** Present passports, immigrant visas, and COPR documents to Canada Border Services Agency officers. Provide Canadian forwarding address for physical PR cards.
- [ ] **Goods Accounting Declaration:** Submit CBSA Form **BSF186 / BSF186A** (*Personal Effects Accounting Document*) listing accompanying luggage and unaccompanied "goods to follow" to secure complete exemption from Canadian customs duty and GST.
- [ ] **Currency Declaration:** If carrying $10,000 CAD or more in cash/travellers cheques/monetary instruments, submit completed CBSA Form **E677**. (No tax or limit, but mandatory reporting).

### 3. First 72 Hours (Core Identity & Communications)
- [ ] **Social Insurance Number (SIN):** Visit a Service Canada Centre in person (e.g., Harry Hays Building, 220 4th Ave SE, or suburban service centres). Present passport and COPR/Work Permit to obtain your 9-digit SIN immediately on official paper.
- [ ] **Canadian Mobile Phone Plans:** Purchase 2 Canadian postpaid SIM/eSIM cards (TELUS, Rogers, Bell, Koodo, Fido, Freedom, Public Mobile). Establishing a Canadian phone number is required for multi-factor authentication for CRA, Alberta.ca, and banking.
- [ ] **Bank Account In-Person Activation:** Visit your designated bank branch with passport, COPR/permit, and SIN to verify identity, activate chequing accounts, collect Interac debit cards, and apply for your first newcomer credit card.

### 4. First 7 Days (Health & Mobility Essentials)
- [ ] **Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan (AHCIP) Application:** Visit an authorized Alberta Registry Agent office in person. Present: (1) Passport, (2) COPR / PR card or valid Work Permit, and (3) Proof of Alberta address (rental lease, bank statement, utility letter). *Note: Coverage begins effective the date residency is established in Alberta with zero waiting period.*
- [ ] **Driver's Licence Class 7 Knowledge Test:** Visit an Alberta registry agent to take the computer-based Class 7 knowledge test (30 questions, 80% pass). Surrender physical Saudi licence and submit certified Moroor driving abstract for Service Alberta Special Investigations Unit (SIU) experience credit.
- [ ] **Transit Passes:** Purchase Calgary Transit monthly pass ($126/adult). Note: Children 12 and under ride 100% free; no ticket or pass required.

### 5. First 30 Days (Permanent Settlement & Schooling)
- [ ] **Secure Permanent Lease:** Execute residential tenancy agreement for a 3- or 4-bedroom home. Pay security deposit (maximum one month's rent by Alberta law).
- [ ] **Mandatory Tenant Insurance:** Purchase tenant insurance with $1M–$2M liability and contents coverage ($25–$40/mo).
- [ ] **Home Utilities Setup:** Establish electricity and natural gas billing with ENMAX Energy or ATCO. ENMAX automatically bundles City of Calgary municipal services (water, sewer, stormwater, garbage/recycling carts).
- [ ] **Home Broadband Internet:** Order high-speed broadband (TELUS PureFibre symmetrical FTTH or Rogers Xfinity cable).
- [ ] **Children School Registration:**
  - *Public CBE:* Book an appointment with the CBE Welcome Centre (1221 8 St SW) via SchoolEngage for ELL assessments and school placement.
  - *Islamic Schools:* Submit applications to Calgary Islamic School (Akram Jomaa / OBK) or Al-Amal Academy.
- [ ] **Canada Child Benefit (CCB) Application:** If landed as Permanent Residents, submit CRA Form RC66 (*Canada Child Benefits Application*) and RC66SCH (*Status in Canada*). *Warning: Work Permit holders are ineligible until 18 consecutive months of Canadian residency have elapsed.*
- [ ] **Primary Care Physician Intake:** Register with clinics accepting new patients via `albertafindadoctor.ca` (Primary Care Networks).

### 6. First 90 Days (Full Integration)
- [ ] **Class 5 Road Test:** Once SIU confirms driving experience credit, book and pass the single Class 5 road test to exit GDL directly to a Full Class 5 licence before the 90-day foreign driving permit expires.
- [ ] **Family Vehicle Purchase & Registration:** Purchase family SUV/van (0% Alberta sales tax advantage). Secure auto insurance using the Saudi claims-free letter, and register licence plates at an Alberta registry agent.
- [ ] **Winter Preparation:** If arriving in fall, purchase a dedicated set of 4 winter tires (Michelin X-Ice Snow or Bridgestone Blizzak) mounted on rims ($1,000–$1,500), engine block heater extension cord, and winter outfitting for all family members.
- [ ] **CRA My Account & Service Canada Digital Accounts:** Set up online access to track tax notices, direct deposits, and official records.

### 7. First Year (Milestones)
- [ ] **First Canadian Tax Return Filing:** File federal and Alberta tax returns by April 30. Declare worldwide income from arrival date onward to establish ongoing CCB, GST/HST credits, and Alberta Child and Family Benefit (ACFB) eligibility.
- [ ] **Credit Score Check & Limit Expansion:** Review Equifax and TransUnion credit reports (via Borrowell and Credit Karma). Request credit limit increases on initial cards to maintain credit utilization <20% and achieve a prime score (720–760+).
