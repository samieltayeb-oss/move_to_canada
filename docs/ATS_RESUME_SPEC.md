# CANADIAN ATS RÉSUMÉ SPECIFICATION
**Standard:** Canadian Applicant Tracking System (ATS) & Human Rights Compliant Format  
**Audience:** Experienced Financial & Operations Professionals  
**Reference:** Canadian Human Rights Act, CPHR Canada Standards  

---

## 1. Statutory & Anti-Bias Compliance Rules
Under Canadian federal and provincial human rights legislation, employers must evaluate candidates strictly on bona fide occupational requirements. A compliant Canadian résumé MUST OMIT:
- ❌ **Photographs / Headshots** (Avoids implicit racial or age bias; many ATS parsers reject photo PDFs).
- ❌ **Date of Birth / Age** (Human rights protection against age discrimination).
- ❌ **Marital Status & Children** (Protection against family status discrimination).
- ❌ **Religion / Faith** (Protection against religious discrimination).
- ❌ **Nationality / Citizenship Details** (Unless stating legal work authorization, e.g. "Authorized to work in Canada — Permanent Resident").
- ❌ **Full Physical Street Address** (City and Province is sufficient, e.g. "Calgary, AB").

---

## 2. Technical Formatting Specifications for ATS Parsers
- **Layout:** Strict **Single-Column** layout (Taleo, Workday, Greenhouse, and iCIMS frequently scramble multi-column tables and text boxes).
- **Fonts:** Clean standard cross-platform sans-serif (Inter, Arial, Calibri, Helvetica) or standard serif (Georgia, Garamond).
- **Headings:** Standard recognized section titles:
  1. Professional Summary
  2. Core Competencies / Technical Skills
  3. Professional Experience
  4. Education & Credentials
  5. Professional Certifications
- **Bullet Structure:** Action Verb + Scope + Quantified Result.
  - *Example:* "Supervised daily trade settlement operations for $1.5B+ institutional fund portfolio, achieving 99.8% on-time settlement rate."
- **Page Budget:** Exactly 2 pages for 7–15 years of experience.
- **Export Options:** Clean plain text (`.txt`), ATS-structured PDF, and Markdown.
