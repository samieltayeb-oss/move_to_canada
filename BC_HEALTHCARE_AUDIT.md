# BRITISH COLUMBIA HEALTHCARE AUDIT (MSP & FAIR PHARMACARE)

## 1. Statutory Framework
- **Legislation:** *Medicare Protection Act*
- **Waiting Period:** MANDATORY statutory waiting period enforced.
  - Formula: **Balance of arrival month + 2 consecutive calendar months**.
  - Example: Arrival on July 15 $\rightarrow$ coverage begins October 1.
  - **CRITICAL DIRECTIVE:** Newcomers MUST purchase private bridge insurance covering emergency medical expenses for the first ~75 to 90 days.
- **Premiums:** $0. Eliminated since January 1, 2020 for citizens, PRs, and work permit holders.
- **BC Services Card:** Mandatory in-person photo/identity verification at an ICBC Driver Licensing Office after HIBC application.
- **HealthLink BC:** 24/7 dial 811 (RNs 24/7, pharmacists 5pm–9am, dietitians, exercise professionals).

## 2. Coverage Exclusions & Fair PharmaCare
- **Outpatient Prescription Drugs:** Not covered automatically. Covered through **Fair PharmaCare (Plan I)**.
  - Requires separate registration with HIBC and CRA income verification.
  - Annual family deductible is 2% to 3% of family net income. Once met, PharmaCare pays 70% and family pays 30% copayment until family maximum (3% to 4% of income) is reached, after which PharmaCare covers 100%.
- **Ambulance:** BC Emergency Health Services (BCEHS) ground ambulance fee is $80 for residents with MSP coverage ($539+ for non-residents/uninsured).
- **Dental & Vision:** Adult routine care excluded from MSP.
