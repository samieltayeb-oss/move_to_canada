# BRITISH COLUMBIA HOUSING & TENANCY AUDIT (2026)

## 1. Tenancy Law & Financial Rules (*Residential Tenancy Act*, BC RTA)
- **Security / Damage Deposit:** LEGAL under BC RTA section 19, capped at a maximum of **50% of one month's rent**.
- **Pet Damage Deposit:** Permitted up to an additional 50% of one month's rent if pets are allowed.
- **Last Month's Rent:** Landlords do NOT collect a separate "last month's rent" upfront in British Columbia.
- **Universal Rent Control:** Annual rent increase cap (3.0% - 3.5%) applies to **all** residential rental units regardless of building age or construction date.
