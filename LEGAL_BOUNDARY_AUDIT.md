## 1. The Statutory Immigration Boundary
Under Canadian law (*Immigration and Refugee Protection Act*, s. 91), only authorized individuals (members in good standing of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants — CICC, or a Canadian law society) may represent or advise individuals on immigration applications for consideration.

NEXORA MOVE strictly honors and reinforces this boundary across all software layers:
1. **No PR Eligibility Determinations:** The software does not calculate CRS scores or evaluate whether a user qualifies for Express Entry or PNP streams.
2. **No Form Completion or Submission:** The platform never fills out or submits immigration documents to IRCC on behalf of users.
3. **Explicit Public Disclaimers:** Visible notices on the Home page, Pricing page, Terms of Service, and dedicated `/disclaimer` route state that NEXORA MOVE is an educational and financial decision-support tool.
4. **Official IRCC Referral:** Users seeking individualized visa representation are actively directed to authorized CICC consultants and the official `Canada.ca` portal.

## 2. Distinction Between Mathematical Models vs. Legal Claims
*   **Statutory Calculations:** Personal income taxes, Canada Child Benefit (CCB), and provincial child benefits (ACFB, OCB, BCFB) are calculated using 100% deterministic published government formulas.
*   **Cost of Living Estimates:** Rent, groceries, fuel, and childcare costs are transparently framed as market estimates synthesized from CMHC and Statistics Canada benchmark tables.
