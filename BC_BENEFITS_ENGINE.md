# BRITISH COLUMBIA BENEFITS ENGINE SPECIFICATION (2026)

## 1. BC Family Benefit (BCFB)
- **Authority:** BC Ministry of Finance / Canada Revenue Agency
- **Maximum Annual Benefit (Tier 1 Base):**
  - 1st Child: $1,750 / yr ($145.83 / mo)
  - 2nd Child: $1,100 / yr ($91.67 / mo)
  - 3rd Child and subsequent: $900 / yr ($75.00 / mo)
  - Family with 3 Children: $1,750 + $1,100 + $900 = $3,750 CAD / yr ($312.50 / mo)
- **Phaseout Thresholds & Reduction Rates:**
  - Threshold: $30,176 CAD AFNI
  - Reduction Rate: 4.0% of AFNI exceeding $30,176 down to guaranteed floor ($775 + $750 + $725 = $2,250 for 3 kids)
  - Second Threshold: $96,562 CAD AFNI (guaranteed floor reduced by 4% until $0)
- **Family of 5 (3 Children) Calculations:**
  - At $91,900 CAD income: Entitled to guaranteed floor = **$2,250 CAD / yr** ($187.50 / mo)
  - At $125,000 CAD income: $2,250 - (($125,000 - $96,562) * 0.04) = $2,250 - $1,137.52 = **$1,112.48 CAD / yr** ($92.71 / mo)

## 2. BC Climate Action Tax Credit (BCCATC)
- **CRITICAL STATUS:** TERMINATED effective April 1, 2025 due to repeal of provincial consumer carbon tax. $0 for 2026 tax year.

## 3. ChildCareBC Programs
- **CCFRI:** Universal provider-based reduction up to $900/mo for infants/toddlers, $545/mo for 3-5 year olds.
- **ACCB:** Income-tested subsidy up to $1,250/mo for families earning under $111,000 CAD.
