# ONTARIO SCHOOLS & ISLAMIC COMMUNITY AUDIT (2026)

## 1. Public & Catholic School Boards
- **Toronto:** Toronto District School Board (TDSB) & Toronto Catholic District School Board (TCDSB). Free newcomer reception at Central Reception Centres.
- **Peel Region (Mississauga & Brampton):** Peel District School Board (PDSB) & Dufferin-Peel Catholic District School Board (DPCDSB). Welcome Centres in Mississauga and Brampton for ELL intake.
- **Ottawa:** Ottawa-Carleton District School Board (OCDSB). Family Reception Centre for ELL.
- **Hamilton:** Hamilton-Wentworth District School Board (HWDSB). Assessment Centre on Main St E.
- **Waterloo Region:** Waterloo Region District School Board (WRDSB).

## 2. Accredited Islamic Schools (Independent Private)
*All independent private schools in Ontario receive $0 provincial operational subsidy; funded 100% by parent tuition and donations.*
- **ISNA High School (Mississauga):** Grades 9-12 (Elementary JK-8 on Sherway Dr). Tuition ~$7,200 – $8,200/yr. Full OSSD + Arabic/Quran.
- **Olive Grove School (MAC - Mississauga):** JK to Grade 8. Tuition ~$6,200 – $7,500/yr. Full Ontario curriculum + Islamic studies.
- **Wali ul Asr Learning Institute (Brampton & Halton):** JK to Grade 12. Scaled tuition with financial assistance guarantee.
- **Abraar School (MAC - Ottawa):** Elementary (Fieldrow St) and Secondary (Grenon Ave). Tuition ~$6,880/yr.
- **As-Sadiq Islamic School (Jaffari Community Centre - Thornhill/GTA):** Preschool to Grade 12. Tuition ~$6,500 – $7,800/yr.

## 3. Mosques & Islamic Community Hubs
- **Toronto:** Islamic Foundation of Toronto (Nugget Ave), TARIC Islamic Centre, Madinah Masjid.
- **Mississauga:** ISNA Canada Islamic Centre (South Sheridan Way), Sayeda Khadija Centre, Al-Falah Islamic Centre.
- **Brampton:** Makki Masjid, Brampton Islamic Centre, Jamiat-ul-Ansar.
- **Ottawa:** Ottawa Islamic Centre (Assunnah), South Nepean Muslim Community (SNMC), Islam Care Centre.
- **Hamilton:** Hamilton Downtown Mosque (HMA), Mountain Mosque.
- **Kitchener-Waterloo:** Kitchener Masjid (Muslim Association of Canada), Waterloo Masjid (Waterloo Mosque).
