## 1. Taxation & Statutory Deductions
- **Personal Income Tax:**
  - 5.05% on taxable income up to $53,891
  - 9.15% on taxable income from $53,891.01 to $107,785
  - 11.16% on taxable income from $107,785.01 to $150,000 (frozen)
  - 12.16% on taxable income from $150,000.01 to $220,000 (frozen)
  - 13.16% on taxable income over $220,000 (frozen)
- **Ontario Surtax:**
  - 20% on Basic Ontario Tax over $5,818
  - 36% on Basic Ontario Tax over $7,446
  - Effective top provincial marginal rate: 20.53% (All-in federal + provincial top rate: 53.53%)
- **Ontario Health Premium (OHP):**
  - Income <= $20,000: $0
  - Income $20k - $25k: 6% over $20k (max $300)
  - Income $25k - $36k: $300
  - Income $36k - $38.5k: $300 + 6% over $36k (max $450)
  - Income $38.5k - $48k: $450
  - Income $48k - $48.6k: $450 + 25% over $48k (max $600)
  - Income $48.6k - $72k: $600
  - Income $72k - $72.6k: $600 + 25% over $72k (max $750)
  - Income $72.6k - $200k: $750
  - Income $200k - $200.6k: $750 + 25% over $200k (max $900)
  - Income > $200,600: $900
- **Basic Personal Amount:** $12,989 CAD (tax credit = $655.94)
- **Harmonized Sales Tax (HST):** 13% (5% federal GST + 8% provincial). Point-of-sale rebates on books, children's clothing/footwear, diapers, car seats, and qualifying prepared food under $4.00.

## 2. Healthcare (OHIP & OHIP+)
- **Waiting Period:** Zero days. Permanently eliminated since March 19, 2020 via Regulation 552 amendment (*Health Insurance Act*).
- **Enrolment:** In-person at ServiceOntario with 3 original documents (legal status, proof of Ontario address, signature ID).
- **Telehealth:** Health811 (24/7, 200+ languages including Arabic).
- **Youth Drug Plan (OHIP+):** 100% covers 5,000+ formulary medications at $0 copay for youth under 25 who are NOT covered by private insurance.
- **Ambulance:** $45 copay for medically necessary transport with valid card ($240 if not medically necessary).
- **Podiatry:** OHIP subsidizes $7 - $16 per visit, capped at $135/yr.

## 3. Driving & Non-Reciprocal Exchange (DriveTest)
- **Grace Period:** 60 days to drive on valid foreign licence.
- **Foreign Experience Credit:**
  - Up to 12 months: Self-declaration on application. Waives 12-month G1 wait; eligible for G2 road test immediately.
  - 12+ months: Requires official driving record (Moroor letter with ATIO translation).
  - *MTO Regulatory Update (July 1, 2026):* Direct G road test bypass capped; applicants must complete G2 test and hold G2 for 12 months before attempting full G test.

## 4. Housing & Rental Benchmarks
- **Tenancy Law:** Security/damage deposits are 100% illegal under Ontario RTA. Landlords may collect first and last month's rent only. Units first occupied post-November 15, 2018 are exempt from provincial annual rent increase caps.
