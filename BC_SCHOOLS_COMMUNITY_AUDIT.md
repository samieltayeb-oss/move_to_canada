# BRITISH COLUMBIA SCHOOLS & ISLAMIC COMMUNITY AUDIT (2026)

## 1. Public School Districts
- **Surrey (SD36):** Largest district in BC (~82,000 students). Surrey Welcome Centre provides settlement workers in schools (SWIS) and ELL assessments.
- **Vancouver (VSB SD39):** ~50,000 students. VSB Newcomer Welcome Centre at Moberly School.
- **Burnaby (SD41):** ~25,000 students. District Language Assessment Centre.
- **Richmond (SD38):** ~22,000 students. Central Registration & Welcome Hub.
- **Coquitlam (SD43):** ~32,000 students (Tri-Cities). SWIS partnerships.
- **Greater Victoria (SD61):** ~20,000 students. S.J. Willis Educational Centre Welcome Hub.

## 2. Accredited Islamic Schools (Ministry Group 1 Funded)
*Under the BC Independent Schools Act, accredited Group 1 independent schools receive 50% per-pupil operating grants from the Ministry of Education, keeping parent fees affordable.*
- **Surrey Muslim School (BCMA - Surrey):** K to Grade 7. Tuition ~$3,957/yr for 1st child (reduced to ~$2,650 for subsequent siblings). Full BC curriculum + Arabic/Tahfeez.
- **Iqra Islamic School (Surrey):** K to Grade 7. Tuition ~$4,275/yr (1st child), $3,800/yr (2nd child).
- **Az-Zahraa Islamic Academy (Richmond):** JK to Grade 7. Tuition ~$3,800 – $4,400/yr. Located on No. 5 Road.

## 3. Mosques & Islamic Community Hubs
- **Surrey:** Surrey Jamea Masjid (BCMA, 124th St), Guildford Islamic Cultural Centre, Fleetwood Musalla.
- **Vancouver:** Al-Jamia Masjid (West 8th Ave, oldest mosque in BC), MAC Vancouver Centre, Masjid Omar Al-Farooq.
- **Burnaby:** Burnaby Mountain Mosque, Masjid Al-Salam & Education Centre.
- **Richmond:** Richmond Jamea Masjid (BCMA), Az-Zahraa Islamic Centre (Shia).
- **Coquitlam / Tri-Cities:** Masjid Al-Hidayah (Port Coquitlam / Coquitlam).
- **Victoria:** Masjid Al-Iman (Quadra St, Victoria Islamic Centre).
