### 1.2 Supermarket Retailer Tiers in Calgary
1. **Costco Wholesale (East Hills / Beacon Hill / Sunridge):** Essential for bulk staples (Basmati rice, flour, cooking oil, paper products, halal frozen items).
   - *Pricing Distinction:* Warehouse floor price is 15%–20% cheaper than Costco.ca or Instacart delivery.
   - *Membership Break-Even:* $65 Gold Star vs. $130 Executive (2% cash back).
2. **Real Canadian Superstore (Westwinds NE / Country Hills):** Primary full-service grocery store carrying certified Halal meats (Sufra, Mina), flatbreads, and international produce.
3. **Discount Grocers (Walmart Supercentre / No Frills / FreshCo):** Competitive weekly loss-leaders and pantry items.
4. **Mid-to-High Tier (Safeway / Sobeys / Save-On-Foods):** Higher everyday retail markups, but provide neighborhood pharmacy access.

## 2. Fuel, Vehicle Economics & Regulations

### 2.1 Fuel Pricing & Commute Calculations
- **StatCan Benchmark:** Verified at **144.9¢/L ($1.449/L)** for Regular Unleaded 87 (September 2026).
- **Costco Gas Bar:** Averaging **136.9¢/L** (approx. 8¢/L discount).
- **NRCan Fuel Consumption Math:**
  $$\text{Monthly Fuel Cost} = \left( \frac{\text{Monthly Kilometers}}{100} \right) \times \text{L/100km} \times \text{Fuel Price/L}$$
- **Vehicle Benchmark Comparison (At 20,000 km/year):**
  - *Toyota Highlander Gas (10.5 L/100km):* 2,100 L/yr → **$3,043 CAD / yr** ($253.58 / mo).
  - *Toyota Highlander Hybrid (6.7 L/100km):* 1,340 L/yr → **$1,942 CAD / yr** ($161.83 / mo) → **$1,101 CAD/yr fuel savings**.
  - *Toyota Sienna AWD Hybrid (6.6 L/100km):* 1,320 L/yr → **$1,913 CAD / yr** ($159.42 / mo).

### 2.2 Saudi Licence Exchange & Vehicle Import Rules
- **Non-Reciprocal Licence Process:** Saudi Arabia does NOT have a reciprocal driver's licence exchange with Alberta.
  - *Steps:* Must surrender Saudi licence at an Alberta registry for verification under the GDL exemption program, pass the Class 7 Knowledge Test and Vision Exam, and complete the Class 5 Road Test.
  - *Required Document:* Official stamped driving history letter from Saudi Traffic Police (Moroor) translated into English to credit past driving experience.
- **15-Year Vehicle Import Ban (RIV / Transport Canada):**
  - Non-US specification vehicles younger than 15 years old cannot be legally imported or registered in Canada due to CMVSS compliance rules.
  - GCC-spec cooling and emissions are not winter-rated.
  - *Recommendation:* Sell vehicle in Riyadh before departure; purchase a certified AWD family vehicle in Calgary (0% provincial sales tax).
- **Winter Tires:** Budgeted at **$1,250 CAD** for a mounted set of 4 winter tires on dedicated rims (Bridgestone Blizzak / Michelin X-Ice).
