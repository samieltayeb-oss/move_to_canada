# ONTARIO HEALTHCARE AUDIT (OHIP & OHIP+)

## 1. Statutory Framework
- **Legislation:** *Health Insurance Act*, Regulation 552
- **Waiting Period:** ZERO DAYS. Revoked March 19, 2020 via O. Reg. 107/20. New permanent residents receive immediate coverage upon approved registration.
- **Application Method:** In-person at ServiceOntario. Mandatory: proof of legal status (COPR/PR card), proof of physical Ontario residency (lease/utility bill/bank statement), proof of identity with signature.
- **Health811:** 24/7 bilingual nurse triage and health navigation with on-demand interpretation in 200+ languages including Arabic.

## 2. Coverage Exclusions & Financial Boundaries
- **Prescriptions (Aged 25–64):** 0% covered by OHIP. Requires private employer insurance or Trillium Drug Program.
- **Prescriptions (Youth under 25):** 100% covered ($0 copay) under **OHIP+** for over 5,000 formulary medications, PROVIDED the youth has NO private insurance coverage.
- **Ambulance:** $45 copayment for medically necessary trips ($240 if not medically necessary).
- **Dental:** Excluded (must use private insurance or CDCP if income < $90k).
- **Podiatry:** OHIP pays only $7 - $16 per visit, capped at $135/yr.
